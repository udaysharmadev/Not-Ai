## Rationale: why everything stayed

- **Nominalization remains:** `implementation/invalidation` name the protocol. Unpacking to verbs would rename a defined thing. Academic/technical genres legitimately keep more (E01 genre caveat).
- **Passive remains:** actor (monitoring) is irrelevant; active rewrite adds words without information.
- **Long sentence remains:** the March clause carries date + requester + cause in one checkable unit. Splitting buries the causal link.
- **Moreover remains:** real additive relation (request -> verification). Deleting it removes the step marker.
- **Em dash remains:** house style permits it here and it is the author's own punctuation, not newly authored decoration. The universal "never add em dashes" is HOUSE_STYLE, not science; this project keeps it for new prose but does not strip protected author choices.
- **No vocabulary swap:** no significance inflation or ceremonial conclusion present; nothing to cut.
