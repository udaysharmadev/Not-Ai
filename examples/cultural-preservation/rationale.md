## Rationale: preserve, do not normalise

Per Agarwal et al. CHI 2025 (E07) and Liang et al. 2023 (E11): constrained/formal and regional varieties are a writer's reality, not a defect. The edit question was "will the manager understand this, and is this how the writer communicates?" — yes on both. Normalising to US casual style would homogenise (E08) and risk reader mismatch (formal request -> casual). `cultural.py:detect_variety()` reports evidence, never nationality.
