Narendra Modi is the prime minister of India. He was born on 17 September 1950 in Gujarat. Before becoming PM he was the chief minister of Gujarat. He became prime minister in 2014 and is now serving his third term. Many people like him for his speeches and the work his government has done like Digital India and Swachh Bharat. But not everyone agrees with his decisions. Some people think policy should address jobs and rising prices. He has been central to Indian politics for many years and people have different opinions about him today.
