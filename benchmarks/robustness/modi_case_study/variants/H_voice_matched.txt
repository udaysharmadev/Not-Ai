Narendra Modi has served as India's prime minister since 2014 and is now in his third term. Born in Gujarat on 17 September 1950, he served as chief minister of Gujarat before becoming prime minister. Many voters back him for his speeches and flagship programs such as Digital India and Swachh Bharat, but not everyone agrees with his decisions. Critics argue that the government needs to do more on jobs and rising prices. After decades at the center of Indian politics, people still hold different opinions about his record today.
