We ran the experiment across three separate days because two other groups shared the laboratory autoclave. On the first day, we prepared 24 agar plates and incubated them at 37 degrees Celsius for 18 hours. Two plates showed edge contamination, so we did not include them in the final count. Across the remaining 22 plates, colony growth slowed once the salt concentration reached 3.5%, matching Tanaka (2021). Under our laboratory conditions, salt concentrations above 3.0% reduced this strain's growth rate.
