The experiment was conducted in three separate days because the laboratory autoclave was shared with two other groups. In the first day, we prepared 24 agar plates and incubated them at 37 degrees Celsius for 18 hours. Two plates were contaminated near the edge, so we did not include them in the final count. In the remaining 22 plates, colony growth was slower when the salt concentration reached 3.5%, which agrees with the result reported by Tanaka (2021). We conclude that salt above 3.0% reduces the growth rate in this strain under our laboratory condition.
