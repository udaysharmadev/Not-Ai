My first winter in Shimla ran on the schedule of the evening power cuts. Around 6:15 p.m., the voltage would drop until the filament bulb above the kitchen table turned a dull orange, and then the house went dark. My grandmother never complained when the heater clicked off; she just pulled the brass kangri closer, poured salted butter tea from the thermos, and quizzed me on my history dates in the dark. Those two-hour blackouts taught me how to keep working when the room goes quiet.
