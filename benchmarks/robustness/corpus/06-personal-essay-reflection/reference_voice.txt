I still keep my grandmother's dented thermos on the shelf beside my desk. When the monsoon knocked out the transformer on Tuesday night, I didn't bother hunting for my phone charger; I lit two candles, sat at the kitchen table, and finished the draft on paper. You don't need three monitors when the deadline is real. You just need a quiet room and a pen that works. We used to spend half of January waiting for the grid to come back at 8:00 p.m., and nobody in our house ever called it resilience. It was just Tuesday.
