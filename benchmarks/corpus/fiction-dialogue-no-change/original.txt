Meera set the brass key on the blotter and waited for the ceiling fan to finish its third click. "You told the magistrate the ledger burned in October," she said, keeping her voice below the rain on the tin roof. Dev did not look up from the carbon copy; his thumb stayed pressed against the torn margin where the 1974 stamp still showed in violet ink. "The annex burned," he replied after a pause... "Nobody asked me which cupboard held the duplicate." Outside, a cycle bell rang twice at the gate, sharp and wet, and then the lane went quiet again.
