The migration script started at 01:00 UTC on Sunday. The script copied 420 gigabytes of user records to the new Postgres cluster. The network link saturated after twelve minutes of transfer. The replication lag climbed to 19 seconds on the secondary node. The on-call engineer throttled the batch worker to 50 megabytes per second. The replication lag dropped below 2 seconds within four minutes. The migration completed at 03:40 UTC with zero lost rows.
