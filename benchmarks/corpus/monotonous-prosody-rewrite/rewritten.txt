We started the migration script at 01:00 UTC on Sunday to copy 420 gigabytes of user records to the new Postgres cluster. Twelve minutes into the transfer, the network link saturated and pushed replication lag to 19 seconds on the secondary node. Once the on-call engineer throttled the batch worker to 50 megabytes per second, lag fell back below 2 seconds within four minutes, and the migration finished at 03:40 UTC with zero lost rows.
